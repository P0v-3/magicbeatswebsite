Who we are

More than a game.
Something you make.

We believe technology is more exciting when you can build it, understand it and play with it.

Magic Beats is a creative DIY technology brand that turns simple electronics into playable rhythm-game controllers. Our first product, the Magic Guitar Kit, uses an ESP32 and physical switches. The Magic Drum Kit and other playful instruments are future concepts—not products for sale yet.

Designed around hands-on learning and rhythm gaming, our kits encourage kids and families to explore basic electronics together—then enjoy using what they created.

Introducing the
Magic Guitar Kit.

One kit for sale today. More ways to make music are on the drawing board.

Only product for sale

Build a guitar.
Control the rhythm.

Assemble an ESP32-powered guitar-style controller with physical switches. Turn button presses into keyboard inputs for compatible PC rhythm games, including osu! once your setup is configured.

What comes in the kit

  • 1 × ESP32 boardThe controller brain
  • 4 × push-button switchesPlayable fret-style inputs
  • Hookup wiresFor the switch connections
  • Guitar-shaped body piecesDIY assembly housing
  • USB cable + build guideFor setup and instructions

Illustrative proposed contents; the final packaged bill of materials and exact ESP32 variant need confirmation before fulfilment.

Meet the
Magic Drum Kit.

The Magic Drum Kit is our next build-and-play idea: a compact rhythm controller where tapping a pad becomes a digital input. Designed as a future hands-on project for makers and families.

Planned kit contents

  • 1 × microcontrollerTo detect taps
  • Piezo drum sensorsTo sense pad strikes
  • Resistors + hookup wiresFor sensor circuitry
  • Drum pad materialsFor the playable surface
  • Assembly and setup guideFor the finished prototype

Concept contents only. Sensor count, protection circuitry, and connections are not final.

Not available to purchase. Design and specifications are still being explored.
